# RabbitHole resume entries

## Recommended entry

**RabbitHole — Research Discovery & ML | Python, SQL, JavaScript, R, MATLAB, Figma**
[GitHub](https://github.com/DaniyalHayat/rabbithole) · [Interactive Figma app](https://www.figma.com/make/AJhaZiufnubi0QBAiJPasT)

- Built an interactive research discovery app connecting **1,598 papers through 2,437 citations**, with explainable maps, paper comparisons, saved reading trails, and a **77-country research atlas** supporting GeoJSON export.
- Implemented **BM25/48-dimensional latent retrieval** and trained a pairwise ML reranker on **13,785 comparisons**; evaluated 300 held-out queries over 5,183 documents and retained the stronger hybrid model at **0.671 nDCG@10**.
- Engineered reproducible **Python/SQL data pipelines** and **64 automated tests**, verified browser/Python ranking parity, and independently replicated graph/statistical analyses in **R and MATLAB**; delivered a responsive runnable Figma app.

Use the test count in the final validation report if additional tests are added. These are project measurements, not production adoption or business outcomes. Do not describe the 0.00546 nDCG difference as statistically significant. The benchmark is biomedical retrieval, not a user study of the curiosity app.

This entry adds software engineering, information retrieval, graph algorithms, and product design to a portfolio that already demonstrates financial modeling, accounting controls, and risk analysis. I would place it first when applying for software, data engineering, data science, search, or technical product roles. I would keep only the most relevant two or three projects on a one-page resume.

R and MATLAB are executed analytical companions in this repository. They validate graph and paired-metric analyses; they do not run the browser frontend. I would only list skills I can explain in an interview.
